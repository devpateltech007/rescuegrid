# Five-minute demo script

Setup before recording: `scripts\restart_backend.cmd` (or EC2 URL), open the app in Chrome at
1920×1080, **Reset** the mission, open **Director** and leave the default operator name.
Every number below is read off the screen during the run. They come from the live graph and
Bedrock, not from a script.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:35 Real problem** | Stage 1 briefing card over the valley. Expand *Documented: Whitson Ave Bridge damage (NCDOT)* on the left. Toggle **NOAA 2024-10-05** off and on. | "Hurricane Helene, September 2024. Buncombe County's after-action report cites 43 storm-related deaths in the county, and roads, bridges and communications failed. In Swannanoa, NCDOT reports flooding washed out both approaches of the Whitson Avenue Bridge and the piles under it. Our user is a FEMA US&R planning specialist who has to find an access route into a valley that ground teams can't reach. This is a historical replay. The drones and some hazards are simulated, and the UI labels every item's provenance." |
| **0:35–1:05 Architecture** | `docs/architecture.md` diagram. | "FastAPI on EC2 runs a deterministic three-drone simulation over real USGS terrain and the real OpenStreetMap road graph. Drone observations go to S3 and then to Amazon Bedrock as image bytes through the Converse API. The output is validated JSON. A human decides, and NetworkX reroutes on the real graph. IAM is least-privilege and logs go to CloudWatch." |
| **1:05–2:15 Swarm** | Director → **Run storyline**. The wide valley shot plays, then the camera follows Bravo. Toggle **Drone Scan / Reconstructed Surface**. Show the drone cards: battery, sector coverage. | "Three drones, three sectors: US 70, the river crossings, and Old US 70. Each lidar scan ray-marches the real DEM plus the modeled storm damage. Points appear only where a drone has scanned. The cyan mesh is the surface rebuilt from 4 m cells. Red cells are where the measured surface departs from the pre-storm baseline." |
| **2:15–3:10 Bedrock** | Auto-pause at the bridge incident. Open **AI inspect**: model ID, latency, tokens, parsed JSON, NOAA evidence image. Open **Incidents**. | "Lidar change detection flagged the bridge approaches. The drone pose selected the georeferenced NOAA frame from 5 October 2024, which is real post-storm imagery. Here's the live Bedrock call: Claude Sonnet 4.5, the latency, the tokens, and the structured output. The model gives no coordinates or road IDs. GIS matching supplies those. The incident is pending review, and the edges are only *suspected* blocked." |
| **3:10–4:00 Reroute** | Tick the evidence box, then **Confirm blocked**. The camera dives to the bridge and pulls back. The bottom panel shows previous vs updated route. | "I confirm from drone evidence. The edges become CONFIRMED_BLOCKED. The router recalculates on the real graph: the old route through Whitson Avenue is invalidated, and the new route goes via Riverwood Road. Distance and time come from the graph, and the times are simulated estimates. Amber segments are still unverified. The coordinator automatically retasked a drone to the Riverwood bridge. The scoring table on the left shows why: a bridge on the route has the highest information gain." |
| **4:00–4:35 Approval** | Resume. When the Riverwood observation arrives, review it, then verify passable or request a re-inspection. **Route & brief** → **Approve**, then **Generate Bedrock mission briefing**. | "The new evidence on the alternate crossing goes through the same Bedrock path, and I decide. Then I approve the simulated route. Bedrock writes the briefing from database facts only, citing the observation and incident IDs. It doesn't calculate routes." |
| **4:35–5:00 Close** | Metrics in the bottom panel and the left overview. `docs/evaluation.md`. | "Measured in this run: area observed, scan points, Bedrock calls and latency, recalculation time in milliseconds. What it doesn't claim: no lives-saved numbers, travel times aren't ETAs, the DEM is pre-storm, and real dispatch requires authorized people. *[team name / organizer-approved contact]*" |

Fallbacks: if Bedrock fails, the red DEGRADED banner appears and no incident is created. Say so, show
**Retry Bedrock** on the observation, and keep going with routing via the API (`POST /api/incidents/...`).
Never present an `OFFLINE_FIXTURE` as live.
